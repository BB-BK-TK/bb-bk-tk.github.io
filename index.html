How I build

I don’t start with a feature. I start by finding the real tension.

I hold a complicated problem long enough to find its simple center. Then I frame the choice, make the smallest useful thing, and learn from what people actually do.

That usually means moving between product, strategy, partnerships, behavior and technology — not treating the screen as the whole system.

Interactive prototype · Fitness + giving

FitGive

Can a tiny workout become a tiny act of good?

FitGive turns 30 guided squats into a 2–4 minute daily ritual with a prosocial reason to start. Users choose their set split and pace, then see participation-based Impact Points grow a shared mission. The current prototype simulates ad and impact data; Impact Points are not cash.

Try the interactive prototype ↗
WIP · Playful behavior · Web + Android

Pause Pet

What if taking a break felt like caring for something?

Pause Pet is a work-in-progress digital wellbeing app built around a small virtual companion and timed focus breaks. Instead of another productivity score or warning, the pet becomes the emotional cue to pause, step away from the screen and come back with a little more intention.

WIP · Preventive behavior · Camera prototype

SunDone

Can a camera make an invisible daily habit visible?

SunDone is a work-in-progress sunscreen habit tool. The prototype uses before/after camera input to explore whether simple visual coverage feedback can make sunscreen application easier to complete and repeat. It is an experimental behavior tool, not a medical or diagnostic product.

Personal builds are independent experiments. They are not Samsung products and should not be read as company roadmaps or endorsements.
