01 · The question

What should happen after the data is collected?

Health platforms are good at showing numbers. The harder product problem is helping someone choose an action that feels relevant, possible, and worth doing today.

One Move started as a technical and product experiment: could live health data be connected safely, interpreted as a current context, and expressed as one small next move rather than another dashboard?

02 · The product thesis

Less dashboard. More direction.

01

Connect

Read available health signals without writing back or changing the source data.

02

Understand

Turn separate metrics into a concise statement about the person’s current context.

03

Act

Recommend one right-sized action and explain why it fits now.

03 · What I built

A real integration first, then the experience around it.

01

Android foundation

Built the app project with Samsung Health Data SDK 1.1.0 and confirmed a successful debug build.

02

Read-only connection

Connected to Samsung Health on a real device using developer mode and a deliberately read-only scope.

03

Step aggregation

Validated the aggregate query and received live step data; one test returned steps = 3486.

04

Guidance prototype

Designed the connected-data summary, contextual message, and a small library of possible next actions.
